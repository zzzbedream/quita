// the problem

Credit life insurance is a black box

In Latin America, credit life cover is compulsory for most consumer lending. The market is profitable, opaque, and the subject of sustained litigation.

⚠

Opaque Loss Ratios

A loss ratio is a disclosure about numbers nobody else can see. Whatever figure the insurer reports, there is no way for the borrower, the regulator, or the market to independently verify it.

💰

Commission Capture

Litigation analysis puts distribution commission as high as 87% of premium. The borrower pays for cover, but on those figures most of the premium never reaches the risk pool.

🔒

Tied Policies

The policy is tied to the loan. The borrower cannot choose a carrier, compare prices, or verify that the cover matches the outstanding debt. This has been litigated extensively in Brazil.

⚙

Quita's Approach

Replace trust with math. Reconstruct the insured balance from Ethereum events proved on Creditcoin. Make every premium and every claim a verifiable on-chain counter. Loss ratio becomes a view function.

On the figures above: market numbers cited here come from analysis of the Tema 972 litigation and industry commentary, and are pending cross-check against public SUSEP statistics. They are presented as an order of magnitude, not as audited data. Nothing in Quita depends on them being exact — the point is that today they cannot be checked at all.

Four events, fully verified

Every event emitted on Sepolia is proved on Creditcoin via the Attestcoin protocol. Receipt status, emitter address, and replay protection are all checked on-chain.

Event Consumer On-Chain Effect
LoanDisbursed LoanMirror Creates the mirrored loan with verified principal
RepaymentMade LoanMirror Updates outstanding balance from outstandingAfter
PremiumPaid PolicyRegistry Credits premium to the capital pool
DeathAttested ClaimEngine Counts toward 2-of-3 attestor threshold
// the key invariant

The payout is never trusted

An attestor who lies about the amount changes nothing. The claim payout is always the minimum of the sum insured and the cryptographically verified outstanding balance.

ClaimEngine.sol 
// The financially consequential quantity is never read from the attestation.
// An attestor who lies about the amount changes nothing.

uint256 outstanding = loanMirror.outstanding(loanId);
uint256 payout = Math.min(policy.sumInsured, outstanding);

pool.transferSettlement(policy.lender, payout);

emit ClaimSettled(loanId, policy.lender, payout, policy.sumInsured);
// trust model

Verified vs trusted

Quita is honest about what it can prove and what it cannot. The financial quantities are cryptographically verified. The real-world fact of death is trusted, with mitigations.

Cryptographically Verified

  • The loan exists and its principal
  • Every repayment and the outstanding balance
  • Premium collected by the pool
  • That the source transaction succeeded
  • That the log came from QuitaOrigin
  • That the proof is not a replay
  • The claim payout amount

Trusted with Mitigations

  • That the borrower actually died
  • Mitigated by 2-of-3 attestor threshold
  • Challenge window before settlement — 24h default, shortened for demo
  • Attestor bonds and slashing on roadmap
  • Death is a world-fact no protocol can verify
// declared limitations

What this is not

Stated here rather than left to be discovered. Each limitation we declare ourselves is worth more than the same one found by a reviewer.

trusted input

Death attestation is trusted

Mitigated by a 2-of-3 threshold and a challenge window, not eliminated. Attestor bonds and slashing are roadmap, not implemented.

mock asset

The stablecoin is a mock

MockStable is a freely mintable 6-decimal ERC20 standing in for a settlement asset. It has no value.

placeholder

The mortality table is a placeholder

Flat monthly rates by age band and sex, declared in the contract as TABLE_SOURCE = "PLACEHOLDER". Plausible magnitudes, not actuarial output. Real use requires BR-EMS 2021 per SUSEP filing.

not a carrier

We are not the insurer

Quita is infrastructure for lenders and insurers. It holds no licence and does not underwrite.

testnet

Testnet only

Sepolia and Creditcoin CC3 Testnet. Attestcoin Writability (Creditcoin → Ethereum) is not used: it is still under third-party testing and audit.

simplified

Premium accrual is simplified

Accrual uses a 30-day month and a single portfolio rate within each band, with no extra premiums. Fine-grained pro-rata accrual is roadmap.
