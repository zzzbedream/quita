What a wallet deliberately cannot do here. Policies are created only by a proof of an Ethereum LoanDisbursed event, so there is no underwrite button — not hidden, absent. Death attestation is restricted to registered attestors and needs a 2-of-3 threshold, and settlement reads the payout from verified state rather than from any caller. The write surface you get is the one that is genuinely permissionless: liquidity.
